Think about the last time you needed a tradie or a local service. You probably searched Google, clicked on a couple of results, and made a judgement call within about 30 seconds. Your potential customers are doing exactly the same thing.

If your business doesn't have a website — or has one that [looks outdated](/blog/how-to-know-if-website-is-outdated) — you're losing that customer before they've even picked up the phone. They move on to the next result. It's that simple.

In a region like the Shoalhaven and Illawarra, where local competition is growing and more businesses are coming online, showing up with a professional, fast-loading website is no longer a nice-to-have. It's the baseline. The same goes for [showing up on Google Maps](/blog/how-to-get-business-on-google-maps) — your listing and your website work hand in hand.

## A Website Works for You 24/7

Your Facebook page is great for engagement, but it relies on people already following you or stumbling across a post. A website is different — it's actively discoverable by anyone searching Google for what you offer, at any time of day.

A plumber in Nowra with a well-optimised website can be getting enquiries at 11pm on a Sunday while they sleep. That's the compounding value of a website that most business owners don't fully appreciate until they have one.

## The Difference Between a Website and a Marketing Asset

There's a big difference between having a website and having a website that actually markets your business.

A basic website that was thrown together five years ago might technically exist, but if it loads slowly, looks broken on mobile, has no clear call to action, and doesn't show up in Google searches — it's not doing anything for you.

A proper website built for your business does several things at once:

- **It builds trust** — a professional design signals that you're a legitimate, established business worth hiring
- **It captures leads** — contact forms, click-to-call buttons, and enquiry funnels mean you never miss a potential customer
- **It ranks on Google** — with the right structure and local SEO, your website becomes the thing that puts you in front of people actively searching for what you offer in your area
- **It works on any device** — most local searches happen on mobile, so a site that looks great on a phone is non-negotiable

## What About Just Using Facebook?

Facebook is a useful tool but it's not a replacement for a website. You don't own your Facebook page — Meta does. Your reach is controlled by an algorithm. And increasingly, customers who find you on Facebook will still Google your business name before they make contact, looking for a website to validate that you're legitimate.

A website and a Facebook page work best together. Facebook drives awareness and engagement. Your website converts that attention into actual leads.
